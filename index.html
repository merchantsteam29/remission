Before you go in

This is a horror game. It has jump scares, the dead, a dead child, and violence. How much of the violence you see is up to you.

You can change this any time in Settings. Play it with headphones: the night is mostly sound.

Map

Only head shots put them down fast. By day they walk. At night the fast ones come out, and they hunt by sight: your flashlight shows them where you are. Gunfire carries for blocks.

The yellow diamond on the compass is where you're going. Supplies (ammo, medkits) are in shops, car parks and on the dead, and the city restocks over time.
